Everything between idea and App Store

One composition for your product pipeline—applications, sprints, focus sessions, analytics, and sync—without the clutter of a project-management suite.

📱

Applications

Catalog every product with lifecycle status, platforms, category, version/build, custom icon, and a linked project folder.

🏁

Sprints & Workbench

Plan sprint work with typed activities—fixes, enhancements, ideas, refactors, research—then reorder and clear them in the Workbench.

⏱

Focus Mode

Double-tap a task to enter Focus. Play/pause a timer, jot notes, mark done, and capture real work sessions automatically.

📊

Analytics

See time totals rolled up by application, sprint, and task so you know where shipping effort actually went.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Keep data on this device only, or sync across your iPhone and iPad with the same Apple ID when iCloud Drive is available.

💾

Backup, Vault & Restore

Export JSON backups, schedule reminders, restore safely, and copy linked project folders into a sequenced Project Vault.

Built for App Store makers

Developer Portfolio is designed for indie developers and small teams who ship on Apple platforms. Customize lifecycle statuses, sprint statuses, platforms, categories, and theme (Light, Dark, or System) to match how you work.
